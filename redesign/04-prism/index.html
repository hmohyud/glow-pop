The real sugar difference

Made with real sugar. On purpose.

Most sugar substitutes are far sweeter than sugar, and that can keep your palate hooked on extreme sweetness, driving more cravings later in the day. So we use a little real sugar instead: just 47 delicious calories a pop.

The label, decoded

Made with

  • Real sugar
  • Collagen peptides
  • Hyaluronic acid
  • Vitamins C, B12 & B6
  • Zinc

Never added

  • Artificial sweeteners
  • Sugar alcohols
  • Stevia
  • Allulose
  • OnoSweet

Gluten-freeFull ingredient list on the pack

  • Just 47 calories

    A little real sugar goes a long way. Each pop is mindfully portioned at just 47 calories.

  • No sweetness creep

    Super-sweet substitutes can train your palate to crave more sweetness all day. Real sugar keeps things balanced.

  • No allulose

    We're watching allulose closely. It's currently banned across the UK and Europe until it's proven safe.

  • No OnoSweet

    It hasn't secured European (EFSA) approval, which still requires extensive toxicology and safety data, so we leave it out.

Always free from ultra-processed fillers

  • Cornstarch
  • Modified potato starch
  • Erythritol
  • Xylitol
