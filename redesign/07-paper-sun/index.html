Collagen peptides

Protein
What it is
Collagen, pre-cut into short chains. That's the "peptide" in Peptide Lollipops.
In your body
Collagen is the most abundant protein in your body. Think of it as your skin's scaffolding. You digest collagen peptides like any protein, and small collagen fragments have even been measured in the blood after eating them.2,3
The research, honestly
Collagen peptides have been studied for skin hydration and elasticity. The research is promising, and still debated.4,5
  • Skin's scaffold
  • Studied for hydration

Fun fact. "Collagen" comes from the Greek kolla, "glue".6

Hyaluronic acid

Sugar chain
What it is
A long sugar chain your body makes itself. About half of your body's hyaluronic acid lives in your skin.7
In your body
It's famous for holding on to water, which makes it central to skin moisture. And your body is busy with it: an adult recycles about a third of their HA every day.7,8
The research, honestly
Oral HA has been studied for skin moisture, mostly in small trials using 120–240 mg a day. Our amount is TBD.9
  • Moisture molecule
  • Studied for hydration
Per pop
mg TBD

Fun fact. Most hyaluronic acid made today is brewed by bacteria.10

Vitamin C

Vitamin
What it is
An essential vitamin your body can't make, so it has to come from food.11
In your body
Your body needs vitamin C to build stable collagen, so the two make a natural pair. Vitamin C contributes to normal collagen formation for the normal function of skin, and to the protection of cells from oxidative stress.12,13
The research, honestly
Most of the skin-tone research on vitamin C is about creams and serums, not eating it. And vitamin C isn't a sunscreen.14
  • Collagen formation
  • Antioxidant

Fun fact. A cup of real strawberries has about 85 mg of vitamin C. (Real strawberries, not the pop.)12

Vitamin B12

Vitamin
What it is
The only vitamin with a metal at its center: cobalt. Hence its other name, cobalamin.15,16
In your body
B12 contributes to normal energy-yielding metabolism, normal red blood cell formation and the normal functioning of the nervous system.13,15
The research, honestly
If you already get enough B12, more won't give you extra energy.15
  • Energy metabolism
  • Red blood cells

mcg TBD

Fun fact. Every bit of B12, in animals and in supplements, was originally made by microbes.16

Vitamin B6

Vitamin
What it is
Not one compound but six related ones. Its active form helps more than 100 enzymes do their jobs.17
In your body
B6 contributes to normal protein and glycogen metabolism and to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue. It also helps your body make serotonin and dopamine.13,17
The research, honestly
B6 has no EU-authorized wording about skin, so we don't claim one. Its authorized wordings are the everyday metabolism jobs above.13
  • Protein metabolism
  • Less tiredness

Fun fact. Chickpeas, potatoes and bananas are all good sources of B6.17

Zinc

Mineral
What it is
An essential trace mineral. Your body has no zinc storage, so a steady daily supply matters.18
In your body
Zinc contributes to the maintenance of normal skin, hair and nails, and to normal immune function. Nearly 100 enzymes need it to work.13,18
The research, honestly
The zinc in mineral sunscreen is a different form doing a different job. Eating zinc doesn't protect you from the sun.19
  • Normal skin
  • Immune function

    What we sift out

    Five things never make it into a Strawberry Pop. Give it a sift, then tap a tag to see why it stays out.

    Tap a tag to see why it stays out.

    what's left: real sugar

    • Artificial sweeteners. None. Not aspartame, sucralose, acesulfame K or saccharin, or any other.
    • Stevia-family sweeteners. No stevia, including the newer fermented kinds (Reb M).
    • Sugar alcohols. No erythritol, no xylitol, no sugar alcohols at all. (Bonus fact: xylitol is dangerous for dogs.25)
    • Allulose. It isn't authorized as a food in the UK or EU yet, and regulators are still reviewing it. So it stays out.21,26
    • Corn and potato starch. Always free from cornstarch and modified potato starch.
    • Falls through: real sugar. Still sugar. We keep it small: 47 calories a pop.

    Fact on a stick

    Pull a pop from the bucket. Every stick has a true thing on it.

    All 12 facts
    1. The word "lollipop" was first recorded in 1784. It may come from an old dialect word, lolly, meaning tongue.28
    2. Hard candy is cooked to the "hard-crack" stage, 300–310 °F, when the syrup is about 99% sugar.29
    3. It cools into a glassy solid with only about 2–3% moisture. That's why humidity makes it sticky.30
    4. The "seeds" on a real strawberry are achenes, and each one is a tiny fruit.31
    5. Scientists have identified at least 28 types of collagen. Type I makes up more than 90% of yours.32
    6. "Peptide" traces back to the Greek peptos, meaning cooked or digested.33
    7. Gelatin, the stuff in classic gummy bears, is partly broken-down collagen. Collagen peptides are cut even smaller.34
    8. Humans, other apes, guinea pigs and some bats all lost the ability to make vitamin C through the same broken gene.35
    9. In 1747, aboard HMS Salisbury, James Lind tested scurvy treatments on 12 sailors. The men given citrus nearly recovered.36
    10. Dorothy Crowfoot Hodgkin mapped vitamin B12's structure with X-rays, part of the work behind her 1964 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.37
    11. Seaside trivia: oysters top the zinc charts.38
    12. There's no such thing as plant collagen. Plants supply building blocks your body uses to make its own.2

    Sun sense

    Pop in hand, SPF on.

    Glow responsibly. Here's the small print for sunny days.

    • Is it sunscreen?

      No. Nothing you eat replaces sunscreen, and vitamin C isn't a sunscreen either.14 Dermatologists recommend broad-spectrum, water-resistant SPF 30 or higher, reapplied about every two hours and after swimming or sweating.42

    • Beach-bag rules

      Keep pops in the shade, out of direct sun and hot cars. Skip the fridge: condensation makes hard candy sticky. Keep the bag sealed and dry.30

      General hard-candy tips. Storage directions on the label: TBD.

    • Savor, don't crunch

      Biting hard candy can chip teeth. Savor it, sip some water after, and brush as usual.43

    Sugar & calories

    Are they made with real sugar?

    Yes. No artificial sweeteners, no sugar alcohols, no stevia and no allulose. Real sugar, at 47 calories a pop.

    How much sugar is in one pop?

    Sugar per pop: TBD g. Please don't work it out from the 47 calories: the collagen adds calories too.

    Why not sugar-free?

    Most sugar substitutes are hundreds of times sweeter than sugar. We'd rather keep sweetness at sugar level, in a small amount. That's our take, not a lab result: the science on whether sweeteners change your sweet tooth is still out.23,24

    What's wrong with allulose?

    Nothing dramatic. It just isn't authorized as a food in the UK or EU yet, and regulators are still reviewing it, so we leave it out.21,26

    Is it keto? Will it break my fast?

    It's a 47-calorie candy made with real sugar, so it isn't designed for keto or strict fasting.

    I'm watching my blood sugar. Can I have one?

    It contains real sugar, so please ask your doctor first.

    Is it bad for my teeth?

    It's a real-sugar hard candy. Have one a day, savor it rather than crunching, sip some water after and brush as usual. Your dentist knows your teeth best.43
