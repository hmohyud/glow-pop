No. 04Real sugarThe sweetness question

The real sugar difference

Made with real sugar.

Most sugar substitutes are far sweeter than sugar. So we use a little real sugar instead — just 47 delicious calories a pop.

No artificial sweeteners, no sugar alcohols, no stevia. Nothing that tastes sweeter than it should.

A little real sugar goes a long way.

  1. a.

    Just 47 calories

    Each pop is mindfully portioned at just 47 calories.

  2. b.

    No sweetness creep

    Super-sweet substitutes can train your palate to crave more sweetness all day. Real sugar keeps things balanced.

  3. c.

    No allulose

    We’re watching allulose closely — it’s currently banned across the UK and Europe until it’s proven safe.

  4. d.

    No OnoSweet

    It hasn’t secured European (EFSA) approval, which still requires extensive toxicology and safety data — so we leave it out.

Always free from ultra-processed fillers

  • Cornstarch
  • Modified potato starch
  • Erythritol
  • Xylitol

Enjoy one Glo Pop a day as part of a balanced, happy lifestyle — glowing starts with feeling good about what you eat.
