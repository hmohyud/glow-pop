    Natural strawberry flavor, and its pink comes from beet root powder.

Questions from the deck chairs.

Everything guests ask us between swims.

Collagen Boost is Model Pops’ strawberry beauty lollipop. It’s sugar free, sweetened with isomalt and stevia leaf extract, with four actives in every 15 g pop: 500 mg of hydrolyzed collagen peptides (types I and III), 10 mg of hyaluronic acid, 60 mg of vitamin C and 5,000 mcg of biotin (vitamin B7). Ten pops come in a bag.

We recommend one pop a day, as part of your beauty routine. Each pop carries 5,000 mcg of biotin, so please read the label’s warning before your first one (it’s just below).

Yes. The label says: WARNING: Biotin may interfere with certain laboratory test results. If you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication, have a medical condition, or are scheduled for lab tests, consult your healthcare practitioner before use. CHOKING HAZARD: Small parts. Not for children under 3 years of age. KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN.

Yes: 0 g of sugar and 0 g of added sugars in every pop, as the label says, and no allulose either. It’s sweetened with isomalt, a sugar alcohol (8 g a pop), and a touch of stevia leaf extract, so each pop has just 16 calories.

Like a really, really good strawberry lollipop. The full list: Isomalt, Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides (Type I & III), Ascorbic Acid, Hyaluronic Acid, Citric Acid, DL-Malic Acid, Natural Strawberry Flavor, Natural Color (Beet Root Powder), Stevia Leaf Extract, Lecithin. The label also says GMO free and gluten free.
